# Healthcare Industry Payments Analytics

A simulated end-to-end analytics engagement for a fictional medical device
manufacturer, using real US public data.

> Disclaimer: Halden MedTech is a fictional client. The engagement is simulated.

## Business Scenario

Client:Halden MedTech, a mid-sized US orthopedic and surgical device manufacturer

Client contact:Dana Whitfield, VP of Commercial Operations

Meeting type:Initial kickoff (no prior documentation shared)


## Initial Problem Statement (Draft)
Dana Whitfield, VP of Commercial Operations at Halden MedTech, says Halden has heard that competitors are “spending heavily” on engaging with physicians (not verified), and that Halden’s compliance team is “a little nervous” about how Halden looks in public data. My working interpretation is that the underlying question is how Halden’s publicly reported physician interactions compare with those of its peers, and whether anything in Halden’s own public profile creates compliance or reputational exposure; I am not yet sure whether these are one problem or two. A dashboard is requested for an upcoming board review, but the decision it should inform has not been stated, and I do not yet know who “leadership” is, which competitors and time period matter, what “where we stand” means, or whether the board date and the “next quarter” deadline are the same. These points must be resolved before any metrics, data sources, or dashboard design are defined.
Confidence in this interpretation: medium on the competitive angle, low on the compliance angle.
